How to book a home showing

Buyers who can request a tour without leaving the site convert faster. Here’s the demo workflow built into this concept.

1. Pick the property first

Lock the address before choosing a time. It prevents double-booking confusion and keeps the form scannable on mobile.

  • Select a sample home from the dropdown
  • Or tap a spotlight card to prefill the property
  • Confirm showing type: in-person, private, or virtual

2. Choose a date agents can honor

Default the date picker to tomorrow and block past dates. Agents rarely need same-hour booking from a cold website visitor.

3. Offer time as chips, not a long list

Slot buttons beat dense dropdowns. Eight options covering morning through evening is usually enough for a demo or small team calendar.

4. Capture contact once

Name, phone and email are enough for a showing request. Optional notes catch gate codes, pets or accessibility needs.

5. Confirm on the page

Show a clear success state. In production you’d sync to a CRM or calendar — this concept only confirms on-page so nothing fake gets emailed.
