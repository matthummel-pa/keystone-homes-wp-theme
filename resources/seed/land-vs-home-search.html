Land vs home search

House shoppers and acreage buyers start in different places. Your filters and blog content should respect that.

If they’re shopping homes

Lead with type, price, beds and baths. Acreage is secondary unless they want hobby space.

  • Payment estimate next to the photo
  • School / commute notes in area pages
  • Showing slots for evenings and weekends

If they’re shopping land

Lead with acreage, access, utilities and buildability language. Price-per-acre helps scanning.

  • Perc / septic status called out early
  • Road frontage and driveway notes
  • Township or sample-area context

Shared tools that help both

  • Map + grid toggle
  • Saved searches / alerts
  • One booking flow for tours
  • Short FAQ with schema markup

SEO tip

Publish separate posts for “homes for sale” intent and “land for sale” intent, then link both to your listings filters and showing scheduler.
